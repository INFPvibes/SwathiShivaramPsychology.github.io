K-means clustering of baseline heart rate variability in youth with IBD

The adaptive calibration model (ACM) describes four prototypical patterns of physiological stress responsivity. How these patterns might be reflected in heart rate variability (HRV) metrics in pediatric samples remains an open question.

I used an exploratory four-cluster k-means analysis of baseline HRV metrics in youth with inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) to examine whether the resulting groupings resembled patterns described by the ACM and by Hüsing et al. (2026).
