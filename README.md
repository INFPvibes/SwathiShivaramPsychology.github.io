# Swathi's Computational Psychology Projects
I am interested in examining psychophysiological mechanisms and pathways of psychopathology in the context of stressful life experiences. 
Inspired by the power of storytelling, I aim to be a clinical scientist storyteller. Through computational modeling, we can tell the quantitative story across development, within individuals, and between groups. 
This page contains the models and tools I have built, designed to bridge the clinical and computational world.
