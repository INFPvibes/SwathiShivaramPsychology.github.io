Parallel Mediation Model of Depressive Symptom Persistence following IBD Diagnosis

In a manuscript recently submited to the Journal of Pediatric Psychology, I modeled pessimism and health anxiety as partial parallel mediators of depressive symptom continuity from diagnosis to follow-up in youth with IBD. The hoeplesness theory posits that depression continuity following a stressful life experience may be explained through negative attributional styles. This model applies the hopelessnes theory to a pediatric population that may be experiencing hightened stress following a new chronic illness diagnosis.

Pessimism and health anxiety are modeled as partial parallel mediators of depressive symptoms from the time of IBD diagnosis (Time 1) to the 6-month follow-up (Time 2).
